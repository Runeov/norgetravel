## Follow-ups found during translation (not yet fixed)

- Northern Lights page: the operator buttons link to `#`. They need real, verified GetYourGuide and Viator URLs (Rules.md section 19) before the page can earn anything.
- Tromsø page: Fjellheisen hours and fares were removed because three statements disagreed; add them back from fjellheisen.no when checked. The Edge and Clarion Collection Hotel With share one price range (1,900 to 4,000 NOK); split once each hotel's range is known.
- `src/data/city-guides/tromso.ts` (fields not rendered today): Bardus Bistro address given as Strandgata 9, probably Cora Sandels gate 4; "Mathallen Tromsø: seven producers under one roof" is probably a restaurant and deli, not a hall.
- `.kilocode/rules/Norgetravel_banned_words.md` gives Tromsø's midnight sun as 18 May to 26 July and polar night as 21 November to 21 January. The site uses 20 May to 22 July and 27 November to 15 January, which match the astronomical dates. The rules file should be corrected.
- Some older English strings on the Tromsø page still use em dashes (operator names, the driving heading, the Fjellstua and Finnmarksløpet lines, the hero image alt).
- Translations were produced by the agent pipeline and checked for structure, numbers and banned words, not read by a native speaker. A native read of the four pages before the 8-week gate is recommended.
