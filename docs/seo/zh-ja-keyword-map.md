# Chinese and Japanese keyword map

Research date: 6 October 2026. Sources: Google autocomplete (zh-CN, zh-TW, ja), Baidu autocomplete, SSB and Norwegian press on guest nights, and a manual check of who ranks for the head terms.

## Why these markets

- Chinese guest nights in Norway: 392,000 in 2025, up 78% on 2024. August 2025 alone: 53,000 (+63%).
- Asian guest nights: about 1.2 million in 2025, up 30%.
- All foreign guest nights: 14.2 million in 2025, up 14%, an SSB record.
- Japan: no 2025 figure found yet. Japanese search demand clusters around aurora, fjords and cost.

## The brand advantage

"NorgeTravel" translates directly to the head term in both languages:

| Language | Brand as written | Head search term | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chinese (Simplified) | 挪威旅行 | 挪威旅游 (bigger), 挪威旅行 | 旅游 is the mainland volume term; 旅行 is the literal brand |
| Chinese (Traditional) | 挪威旅行 | 挪威旅遊, 挪威自由行 | Taiwan and Hong Kong search on Google |
| Japanese | ノルウェー旅行 | ノルウェー旅行, ノルウェー 観光 | Exact match between brand and keyword |

Search engines do not translate the domain name, so "norge" in the URL gives no ranking help for 挪威 or ノルウェー. What we control is the brand in titles, site name and headings. The brand is written "Norge Travel" (two words, so it matches the query "norge travel"); the zh site uses "Norge Travel 挪威旅行" and the ja site "Norge Travel ノルウェー旅行".

## Who ranks now

- Chinese: visitnorway.com/cn, KKday, Klook, Trip.com, tour operators (Toursforfun, Wing On), and Taiwanese bloggers. On Baidu, Mafengwo, Ctrip and Qyer dominate.
- Japanese: JTB, HIS, Hankyu, Nippon Travel Agency, 4travel, Trip.com Japan, TravelBook, and personal blogs on note.com.

## Platform limits

- Baidu rarely ranks foreign-hosted sites without a Chinese ICP licence and mainland hosting. Treat Baidu as a bonus, not the target.
- Simplified Chinese on Google reaches Singapore, Malaysia, overseas Chinese and mainland users on VPN. Traditional Chinese on Google (Taiwan, Hong Kong) is a separate market and needs a zh-Hant version.
- Mainland Chinese travellers do much of their planning on Xiaohongshu, Mafengwo and Ctrip, not on search engines.
- Japanese search is Google (Yahoo! Japan uses Google results), so a well-built ja site can compete directly.

## How the site handles translation

- `src/lib/i18n-seo.ts` lists the static pages that have a full zh or ja version (`TRANSLATED_PATHS`). Pages not listed serve English under /zh/ and /ja/, so they point their canonical at the English URL and stay out of the sitemap. Add a path there once its copy is translated.
- Articles count as translated when the body in `articles_zh.json` or `articles_ja.json` is written in Chinese or Japanese (`src/lib/article-locales.ts`). A Japanese body is used only while it is at least as new as the English article.
- `brandTitle()` normalises the brand in every page title: "| Norge Travel" on English pages, "| Norge Travel 挪威旅行" and "| Norge Travel ノルウェー旅行" on the others.
